# Key Findings

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Cases | 507 |
| Peak Year | 2021 (209 cases) |
| Positive Rate | 59.6% (302 cases) |
| Female Patients | 54.4% (276) |
| Male Patients | 45.6% (231) |
| Average Patient Age | 30.4 years |

## Conclusion
Malaria remains a year-round public health burden in Jos.
The 2021 peak and consistently high positivity rate suggest
the need for sustained prevention and diagnostic efforts
beyond seasonal intervention windows.
